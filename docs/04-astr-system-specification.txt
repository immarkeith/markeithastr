1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASTR is a quantitative, factor-based stock scanning, research, and execution platform
designed to operate indefinitely on a strictly $0.00 external cost infrastructure (no paid
subscriptions, no paid API keys).

Key Operational Tenets:
1. Strict Separation of Concerns (Air Gap): Paper/research simulation and live brokerage
   execution are completely segregated. Research findings, simulated trades, or experimental
   algorithms can NEVER touch, mutate, or risk real broker funds without explicit human confirmation.
2. No Paper Broker APIs: Paper trading through third-party broker endpoints (e.g., Alpaca Paper)
   is obsolete and permanently eliminated. All research simulations run internally against
   immutable database ledgers and authentic historical price bars.
3. Human-in-the-Loop Live Firewall: Live order placement that increases capital exposure
   requires an explicit user-confirmation token ("manual_confirmed") from a UI dialog. Automated
   scans can issue notifications or alert cards, but NEVER execute a live market BUY autonomously.
4. Capital Protection & Calendar Realism: Automated cycles protect capital by exiting SAME_DAY
   positions before the market close (preventing overnight gap risk) while honoring protected
   LONG_TERM multi-day positions. All cycles strictly respect New York Stock Exchange calendar
   rules (holidays, weekends, 9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET market hours, and T+1 cash settlement).


2. USER INTERFACE & NAVIGATION ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application is structured into a streamlined, high-density financial dashboard
operating on a dark minimal aesthetic. Navigation consists of eight core tabs:

1. Home (Dashboard):
   - Account Overview: Total Assets, Total Profit, Available Buying Power, and Settled Cash.
   - Bot Status Toggle: Quick Start/Stop toggle for background scanning routines.
   - Market Status Bar: Live status (Market Open, Closing Soon, Closed, Weekend/Holiday) with
     time countdowns to next market events.
   - Stocks Currently Held: Unified list showing live holdings with owning researcher identity,
     target holding duration (e.g., "Trend · 17D target" or "Rotation · Same day"), market value,
     elapsed holding time, and unrealized P&L %.
   - Equity Curves: Interactive P&L chart with range selectors (Today, 1W, 1M, 3M, YTD, 1Y, 5Y, All).

2. Research Lab:
   - Specialist Research Cards: Independent performance tracks for all six specialists and Lead.
   - Normalized Performance Curves: Percentage-based equity curves (preventing distortion from
     differing dollar sizes) updated across time horizons.
   - "Show Me Why" Diagnostic Drawer: Expandable breakdown of formula weights, raw factor scores,
     and parameter sensitivity curves.
   - Parameter Stability Engine: Real-time sensitivity checks verifying formula robustness
     against ±2-day lookback perturbations and ±5% threshold shifts.
   - Market Regime Matrix: 9-state macroeconomic condition grid classifying specialist win rates
     and profit factors under prevailing volatility and trend regimes.

3. Analyze Strategy:
   - On-Demand Single-Stock Scoring: Instant analysis of any user-entered ticker (e.g., AAPL, DIS).
   - Event Intelligence Banner: Scans and surfaces point-in-time SEC 8-K filings for the ticker,
     highlighting filing items, sentiment scores, and direct clickable links to sec.gov.
   - Independent Specialist Ranking Table: Ranks all specialists by historical setup fit against
     the specific searched ticker, displaying optimal holding periods (e.g., 3D, 17D, 3M).
   - Minimum Qualification Gate: Alerts the user if even the top-ranked specialist fails its
     calibrated qualification bar, recommending a PASS rather than forcing a trade.
   - Direct Manual BUY Action: User specifies dollar size ($) and confirms live order placement
     directly attributed to the chosen specialist.

4. Audit Desk (Lead Quantitative Self-Audit):
   - Master Verification Banner: Displays real-time status (ALL PILLARS VERIFIED / ADVISORY WARNINGS /
     MATHEMATICAL VIOLATIONS DETECTED).
   - 4-Pillar Scorecard: Real-time verification of Raw-Bar Reconstruction, Scoreboard Math,
     Balance Integrity ($0.00 leak tolerance), and Look-Ahead Bias.
   - Audit Discrepancy Ledger: Granular table detailing any flagged trade ID, expected vs. actual
     values, and exact mathematical violation reasons.
   - "Run Audit Now": Manual trigger for immediate quantitative self-audit.

5. Scoreboard:
   - Comprehensive performance leaderboard comparing all research strategies, historical win rates,
     average holding days, profit factors, and total realized gains.

6. Trade Log:
   - Immutable audit trail of every closed and open trade, containing fill timestamps, entry/exit
     prices, hold durations, status notes, and owning strategy IDs.

7. Alerts (Notifications):
   - Notification center for scan completions, qualification alerts, market cycle events,
     and audit discrepancies.

8. Settings:
   - Live Alpaca API credential status, system parameters, auto-run toggles, and risk constraints.


3. RESEARCH DEPARTMENT: SPECIALIST & LEAD LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASTR employs an ensemble of independent mathematical researchers. Each specialist has an
isolated mathematical objective, evaluates candidate securities independently, and never
shares or pools calculations with peers.

1. Lead Researcher (Master Formula & Synthesizer):
   - Objective: Multi-factor synthesis, overarching universe ranking, and system-wide self-audit.
   - Role: Blends core momentum, value, stability, and volume factors into the master benchmark
     score. Serves as the primary reference score in the Analyze tab and oversees the Audit Desk.

2. Trend Researcher:
   - Objective: Trend persistence and moving average alignment.
   - Mathematical Focus: Moving average ribbon slopes (SMA20, SMA50, SMA200), higher-high progression,
     and breakout continuation. Favors medium-to-longer holding horizons (e.g., 10D to 45D).

3. Contrarian Researcher:
   - Objective: Mean-reversion from oversold extremes.
   - Mathematical Focus: RSI(14) deep pullbacks (< 30), price extension below lower Bollinger Bands,
     and volume capitulation near major historical support levels. Favors short rebound horizons (1D to 5D).

4. Consistency Researcher:
   - Objective: Smooth equity curve generation and low-variance performance.
   - Mathematical Focus: High Sharpe/Sortino ratios, low maximum drawdown, tight daily return standard
     deviation, and steady positive drift. Favors moderate holding horizons (5D to 20D).

5. Growth Researcher:
   - Objective: Aggressive momentum velocity and volume acceleration.
   - Mathematical Focus: 5-day and 20-day Rate-of-Change (ROC), relative volume surges (RVOL > 2.0),
     and high upside volatility. Favors high-turnaround momentum windows (2D to 10D).

6. Risk Researcher:
   - Objective: Downside containment and asymmetric risk-to-reward ratios.
   - Mathematical Focus: Average True Range (ATR) volatility contraction, tight stop-loss boundaries,
     and favorable risk/reward ratios (minimum 2.5:1 potential gain vs. risk).

7. Skeptic Researcher (Adversarial Validator):
   - Objective: System stress-tester and disqualifier.
   - Mathematical Focus: Actively searches for reasons NOT to trade (liquidity traps, overhead
     resistance, distribution patterns, regime headwinds).
   - Boundary Rule: Excluded from BUY ranking tables. Acts purely as an internal hurdle/critic.

8. Momentum Factor (Analyze-Only Engine):
   - Serves as an analytical benchmark factor across universe ranking without running an isolated paper book.

9. Autonomous Entertainment & Events Researcher (Phase H Satellite):
   - Objective: Discover correlations between real-world events (sports, concerts, movie releases,
     corporate sponsorships) and related equities.
   - Boundary Rule: Strictly isolated research-only layer. Zero authority to modify live trading,
     allocation, or existing specialist formulas without explicit user approval. Operates via $0
     public sources (SEC 8-K, Wikidata, public event feeds).


4. THE STOCK-SPECIFIC "ANALYZE" WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a user evaluates any ticker on the Analyze tab:
1. Historical Retrieval: The system loads full historical daily bars for the ticker (from local
   cache or on-demand free sources).
2. Multi-Horizon Simulation: Each specialist independently tests its unique mathematical formula
   across multiple forward holding horizons:
   - Same-Day (0 sessions)
   - 1 Trading Day (1D)
   - 3 Trading Days (3D)
   - 5 Trading Days (5D)
   - 10 Trading Days (10D)
   - 1 Month (21D)
   - 3 Months (63D)
3. Best-Fit Determination: Each specialist identifies its most reliable historical horizon for
   that specific stock, generating a Stock-Specific Fit Score, sample count, win rate %, and average return %.
4. Fit Ranking: The UI ranks specialists from best fit to least recommended.
5. Minimum Qualification Threshold: Each specialist compares its score against its own evidence-calibrated
   hurdle. If the #1 specialist fails to qualify, the system displays an explicit warning advising the user
   to PASS (avoiding auto-cascading to #2).
6. Execution: The user reviews evidence, selects the preferred specialist row, enters a dollar amount,
   and clicks BUY. A native confirmation prompt proves human intent, and the live order is submitted
   to the broker with the position locked to the selected specialist and holding target.


5. LIVE TRADING, PORTFOLIO RULES & LIVE FIREWALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Live Broker Integration:
- Alpaca Live API (Strictly one live credential pair: ALPACA_LIVE_KEY_ID / ALPACA_LIVE_SECRET_KEY).
- Fractional Shares: Dollar-based orders (e.g., $50.00) submit fractional market orders.
- Execution Firewall: The server function `buyNotional` strictly enforces `origin === "manual_confirmed"`.
  Any programmatic or automated attempt to execute a live purchase throws an immediate security exception.

Position Management:
- SAME_DAY Positions:
  * Designed for intraday factor exploitation.
  * Hard Rule: Never held overnight. Automatically flagged and exited during the 3:45 PM ET pre-close cycle.
- LONG_TERM / Specialist-Assigned Positions:
  * Assigned a specific planned horizon (e.g., 17 trading days or 3 months).
  * Protected Rule: Permanently shielded from automated daily sell cycles. Sells are executed manually
    by the user or upon reaching the defined target/stop conditions with explicit user consent.

Daily Execution Schedule (New York Time):
- 8:00 AM ET: Key-Check cron verifies Alpaca live credential validity and connectivity.
- 9:30 AM ET: Market Open. Live price streaming and active position monitoring commence.
- 3:45 PM ET (Pre-Close Cycle): Scans all active positions; cleanly liquidates all SAME_DAY holdings.
- 5:30 PM ET (Daily Post-Close Cycle): Caches closing bars, re-scores universes, evaluates paper
  research horizons, runs event intelligence, and executes the Lead Quantitative Self-Audit.


6. QUANTITATIVE SELF-AUDIT ENGINE (LEAD RESEARCHER DESK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To guarantee that backtests, simulated paper records, and reporting metrics never suffer from
computational drift, data bugs, or look-ahead bias, the Lead Researcher executes an automated
mathematical audit across four pillars:

Pillar 1: Raw-Bar Trade Reconstruction
- Verifies every simulated trade fill against authentic historical session bars (daily_bars).
- Invariant 1: Entry price must fall within the authentic session bounds: [low * 0.995, high * 1.005].
- Invariant 2: Exit price must fall within the authentic session bounds of the exit date.
- Invariant 3: SAME_DAY trades must have exit_date === entry_date (zero overnight holds).

Pillar 2: Immutable Scoreboard Recalculation
- Bypasses cached counters and recalculates win rates, total P&L, and profit factors directly
  from raw immutable trade records (`trades` table where status = 'closed').
- Compares calculated values against live scoreboard caches to detect and alert on reporting drift.

Pillar 3: Closed-Loop Balance Reconciliation ($0.00 Leak Tolerance)
- Enforces the universal accounting equation:
  Starting Capital + Net Realized P&L + Unrealized P&L = Current Book Value
- Verifies that zero simulated cash leaks, duplicates, or vanishes across paper books.

Pillar 4: Look-Ahead Bias Validation
- Audits historical signals to verify that input datasets used timestamps strictly on or before
  the signal evaluation date (`history_end <= signal_date`).
- Zero data from future bars may ever leak into historical evaluations.

Audit Verdict Hierarchy:
- FAIL: Any mathematical violation (fill outside bar bounds, overnight hold on same-day trade, cash leak).
- WARNING: Missing data bars or advisory anomalies.
- PASS: 100% mathematical integrity across all audited tracks.


7. MARKET REGIME MATRIX & STATISTICAL RIGOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Market environments dictate strategy success. ASTR classifies market conditions into
exactly 9 composite states based on daily SPY analysis:

Regime Engine:
- Trend Dimensions (3 states):
  * Bull: SPY close > SMA200 and 20-day SMA50 slope > 0.
  * Bear: SPY close < SMA200 and 20-day SMA50 slope < 0.
  * Sideways: Mixed moving average alignment.
- Volatility Dimensions (3 states):
  * Realized Volatility over 20 sessions (RV20) benchmarked against the trailing 252 sessions.
  * Classified as Low Volatility (< 33rd percentile), Normal Volatility, or High Volatility (> 67th percentile).
- Precedence Rule: High volatility takes precedence to protect against false breakouts during market shocks.
- Total Regimes: 3 Trends × 3 Volatilities = 9 Composite Regimes.

Statistical Safeguards:
- Point-in-Time: The current session always evaluates using yesterday's finalized regime (zero look-ahead).
- Transaction Costs: An explicit 0.10% round-trip slippage/commission cost is subtracted from all backtests.
- Multiple Testing Correction: Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate (FDR) is applied to prevent
  spurious correlations from being labeled as viable alpha.


8. T+1 CASH SETTLEMENT & CAPITAL TRANCHING (PHASE F ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To ensure 100% realism in cash account trading without triggering broker Good Faith Violations (GFVs):

1. Settlement Calendar:
   - Cash from sold positions is locked in an unsettled pool for exactly 1 trading day
     (`available_on = exit_date + 1 trading session`). Weekends and market holidays do not settle.
2. Tranche Capital Allocation:
   - For daily trading specialists (e.g., 1-day holds), capital is divided into Tranche A and Tranche B:
     * Monday: Tranche A buys.
     * Tuesday: Tranche A sells (funds unsettled until Wednesday); Tranche B deploys settled cash to buy.
     * Wednesday: Tranche A cash is settled and redeployed; Tranche B sells.
   - Eliminates cash exhaustion and guarantees continuous, compliant daily execution.
3. Turnaround-Adjusted Ranking:
   - Capital efficiency is evaluated by duration: Return % / (Hold Days + 1).


9. CENTRAL CALIBRATION & DURATION POOL ROUTING (PHASE G ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Independent Signal Discovery:
   - All 7 formulas autonomously submit qualifying opportunities. Specialists are never forced
     to participate; submitting zero candidates is a valid, respected defensive stance.
2. 6-Pillar Calibration Scoring:
   - Opportunities are scored on: Historical Hit Rate, Current Regime Fit, Setup Quality,
     Reward/Drawdown Asymmetry, Whipsaw Penalty, and Turnaround Efficiency.
3. Cash Protection Cutoff:
   - If no candidate setup clears the minimum calibrated confidence threshold, the system enforces
     "Protect 100% Cash" ($0 deployed).
4. Dual Duration Pools (Short-Term vs. Long-Term):
   - Rather than dividing capital by specialist name, capital is routed into two duration pools:
     * Short-Term Pool: Opportunities with holding horizons of 1–3 trading days.
     * Long-Term Pool: Opportunities with holding horizons of 4+ trading days.
   - Non-Cannibalization Rule: Unspent capital in one pool never drifts or subsidizes the other.
