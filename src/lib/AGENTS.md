- Research Lab graphs plot saved rows from `researcher_daily_ledger` (written by `runResearcherLedger` in the daily cycle); never rebuild researcher curves from trades at render time — graphs must reflect what researchers actually recorded.
- Phase L autonomous live trading lives in `src/lib/autonomous.ts` (pure, tested rules) and `src/lib/autonomous.server.ts`; it uses position_type `AUTO_TIMED` so the same-day pass and long-term protections never touch it, and `buyNotional` re-reads the DB switch for the `autonomous_authorized` origin — why: no caller can self-authorize a live buy.
- Researcher picks live in `research_daily_selections` (insert-only, DB trigger blocks edits) and outcomes in `research_selection_evaluations` (pending rows mature once; final rows locked), written by `runSelectionReplay` in `selection-replay.server.ts` — why: forward results must never rewrite the original pick.
- Lab selection metrics use paginated Layer 2 evaluations via `selection-evaluations.server.ts` in both snapshot rebuild and incremental refresh; only matured, date-valid outcomes count, and absent horizons are unavailable, not pending — why: selection results must remain independent of strategy books and cannot imply nonexistent evidence.
- Paper outage helpers quarantine proven-invalid stale entries without exits or settlement; normal close and same-day evaluation require verified session-range entries — why: an authentic exit cannot turn an impossible entry into trustworthy profit.

- Historical selection charts use versioned `lab_history` snapshots from immutable picks and paginated horizon-1 outcomes after nightly replay, not the legacy prototype; ordinary strategy curves retain their ledger — why: selection accuracy must never impersonate trading-book performance.

- The saved Lab snapshot (`rebuildLabGraph` / intraday refresh) stores ledger curves via `mergeLedgerCurves`, and the ledger reads `scan_input` in pages — why: one long read hits the statement timeout and trade reconstruction is row-capped.
